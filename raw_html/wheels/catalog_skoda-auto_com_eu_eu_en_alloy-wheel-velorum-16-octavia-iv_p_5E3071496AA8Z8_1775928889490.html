Light alloy wheels from the Škoda Original Accessories line-up are available in a wide range of sizes, designs and colours. This allows them to underline the uniqueness of a particular Škoda model and distinguish it from others.
The wheels meet the highest standards of quality and safety, contributing to better handling of the vehicle. Light alloy wheels have the advantage of reduced weight and effective ventilation. The larger space between their spokes enables better cooling of the brakes and a more efficient braking performance.

The compatibility of the wheels and tyres with a specific vehicle needs to be confirmed in the vehicle’s technical documentation.
